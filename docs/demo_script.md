# Smart Hospital Financial Intelligence — 3-Minute Live Demo Script

**Role:** Hospital Chief Financial Officer (CFO) / Finance Lead  
**Timing:** 2:30 – 3:00 Minutes  
**Core Mental Model:** *Source of Truth → Intelligence → Decision*

---

### Step 1: Open on Executive Dashboard (0:00 – 0:40)
- *"Good morning judges. Hospitals know how much they billed, but they struggle to catch where they are losing money and whether major capital expenditures will actually deliver institutional return."*
- *"This is **Smart Hospital Financial Intelligence (AegisFinance)** — a financial decision-support platform designed specifically for hospital leadership."*
- *(Point at KPI cards)* *"Here in our Executive Overview for Metro Multispeciality Hospital, we track total revenue (`₹1.46 L`), operational costs (`₹8.17 L`), and net margins in real time, backed directly by reconciled EHR ledger data."*

---

### Step 2: Highlight the Revenue Leakage Audit (0:40 – 1:25)
- *(Point to the yellow Audit Discovery strip)* *"Notice this warning banner: **₹32,600 in potential revenue leakage** detected across 10 unbilled clinical procedures."*
- *(Click on **Leakage Discovery** tab)* *"Our automated reconciliation engine compares completed clinical services from patient charts directly against final billed invoice line items."*
- *(Spotlight Admission #a7)* *"Look at Admission `a7` for patient Haley Perkins: a completed **MRI Scan worth ₹18,500** was performed in Radiology but omitted from the discharge invoice."*
- *(Click **Add to Bill**)* *"With 1 click, our system generates a supplementary invoice line item, instantly recovering ₹18,500 back into top-line revenue without any manual audit overhead."*

---

### Step 3: Capital Expenditure & ROI Simulator (1:25 – 2:15)
- *(Click on **ROI Simulator** tab)* *"Now, say hospital management is deciding whether to approve a major diagnostic equipment purchase — like a new **3T MRI Scanner**."*
- *(Show Capital Parameters)* *"We input the CapEx investment (`₹2.40 Cr`), expected volume (`450 scans/month`), price per scan (`₹8,000`), and monthly maintenance (`₹18.00 Lakh`)."*
- *(Point at Output Cards)* *"The math engine instantly computes an **accelerated break-even horizon of just 1.11 years (13 months)** with an annualized **ROI of 90.0%** and an annual net contribution of **₹2.16 Cr**."*
- *(Adjust Volume / OpCost Slider)* *"We can stress-test adoption scenarios in real time. If scan volume dips by 10%, the 5-year cumulative cash flow and payback timeline recalculate live."*

---

### Step 4: AI Financial Copilot & Revenue Forecast (2:15 – 2:45)
- *(Switch to **AI Copilot**)* *"Leadership can query the financial copilot for instant synthesis. When we ask *'Where is revenue leaking?'* or *'Should we buy another MRI unit?'*, the engine provides deterministic, mathematically verified guidance grounded in `seed.json`."*
- *(Switch to **Revenue Forecast**)* *"Finally, our trajectory model forecasts forward runway post-leakage reconciliation, showing healthy top-line recovery into Q4."*

---

### Step 5: Close with the Pitch (2:45 – 3:00)
- *"Smart Hospital Financial Intelligence bridges clinical operations with CFO-level clarity — detecting leakage, de-risking capital investments, and protecting hospital margins."*
- *"Thank you, and we welcome your questions."*
